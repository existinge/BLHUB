# BL Voice Acting × Japanese-facing Creative Work

Research date: 2026-10-15 (America/New_York)

## Objective
Find genuine current or upcoming English-language voice acting, dubbing, narration, localization, audio drama and indie game collaborations connected to boys' love (BL), queer romance or Japanese creators/audiences. Candidate is **fluent in English**, studies Japanese but **does not claim Japanese fluency**; remote, beginner-friendly, paid preferred but short portfolio-building volunteer projects considered. No AI-generated drama, synthetic performances, unauthorized voice cloning or voice training.

## Critical distinction
* **Japanese-facing** includes a Japanese creator, Japanese audience, Japanese-themed production or localized Japanese game **with English performance**. This is a priority, not a fallback.
* Japanese native-level casting is NOT assumed suitable. Flag exact requirements and whether scripts, transliterations, pronunciation coaching or bilingual direction are provided.
* A jam project is not an open audition. Completed June–August 2026 Yaoi Game Jam is a creator-discovery lead only.
